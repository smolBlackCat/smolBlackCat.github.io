About the Author

Hello, I am "smolblackcat". I am a computer science student exploring new concepts and always looking to have fun solving computational and mathematical problems.

Since I was young, I have always found myself engaged in learning new technologies. I remember the days when I used to help my father maintain computers. I grew up watching the evolution of operating systems—Windows 7/8/10/11, as well as Mac operating systems.

Objectives and Goals

As a computer science student, my routine is centred around my studies; as well as the concepts learnt at university, I also carry out occasional research to solidify existing concepts and apply them to my routine as a student and developer.

Computer science is a vast field that offers multiple career opportunities. Within this field, I invest my time in research and case studies on the implementation of operating systems—especially UNIX-based systems—and their interfaces, alongside computer network-based architectures.

By the end of my university degree, I aim to invest my time in the maintenance of open-source software systems and creating programmes for the Linux desktop.

Currently...

I am currently absorbing more knowledge about software engineering and putting into practice some new things I have learnt at university. Meanwhile, I also maintain a few open-source projects (one of which is quite well known).
